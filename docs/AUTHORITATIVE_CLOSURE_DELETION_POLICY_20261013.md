# WhatEatO authoritative closure/relocation deletion rule — owner update 2026-10-13

## Binding policy
1. Where the **exact permit management number** from the official local-government food-business licensing record has a confirmed closure date, permit cancellation, or completed relocation/termination at the old address, remove that *specific old-location restaurant* from the active WhatEatO catalog and the unnecessary DB content. **Do not require confirmation from Google or Naver Maps.**
2. An independently verified relocation is a new/updated site record. Never move the old business row to a different address solely by name; distinguish branch and permit identity.
3. Treat a designation cancellation (e.g. 모범음식점 지정취소), aggregator site's '폐점' marker, absence from search or a newer venue of the same name as a **lead**, not as verified official permit termination.
4. Before destructive DB operations: exact UUID, `sbiz_source_key`, last known address, official record type, official closure or termination date, raw URL/evidence, review timestamp. Constrain UPDATE/DELETE by BOTH uuid and source key and verify a single matched record. Never issue name-based bulk deletion.
5. Once official closure is proven, delete from active working restaurant data along with dependent metadata safely, with a concise retained audit log containing only permit key, deletion reason/date and source. Distinguish data cleanup from user review handling; do not silently erase genuine user reviews.
6. Initial 4 cases: records `localdata:3000000-101-1999-10400` (두레유), `localdata:3000000-101-2004-00540` (신선원), `localdata:3000000-101-2009-00417` (copper coffee), `localdata:3010000-101-2021-00317` (살구골). Official termination date not directly verified as of 2026-10-13 for these exact permits, so deletion is **not yet authorized under this criterion**. The 2023-11-29 '폐업' in https://fmr.purpleo.co.kr/view/99 pertains to cancellation of a model restaurant designation, whereas that same source lists operational state '영업'. Do not conflate these.
7. The three 두레유 rows have separate keys and addresses (1999 Pyeongchang30-gil 28, 2013 Bukchon-ro 65, 2023 Pyeongchangmunhwa-ro 84). No deletion by matching name.
8. Production untouched until official record-based delete list is established and separately QA'd. This change supersedes the earlier dual Google/Naver nonlisting requirement for **officially confirmed** closed/relocated records.
